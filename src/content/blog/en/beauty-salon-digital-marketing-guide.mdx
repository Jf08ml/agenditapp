Colombia has more than 180,000 registered beauty salons and barbershops *(estimates from FENALCO and Colombia's Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Tourism)*, and that figure doesn't include informal businesses. Competition in the sector is brutal, especially in big cities like Bogotá, Medellín and Cali. In that context, doing good work isn't enough: you need the right clients to find you, choose you and come back.

Digital marketing changed the rules of the game for local businesses. Tools that used to be reserved for big brands are now available to any salon in Colombia, and many of them are free. The difference between a salon that grows and one that just survives is, to a large extent, knowing how to use them.

This guide covers everything, from how to rank first on Google to how to turn your clients into ambassadors who bring in new ones. Use it as a roadmap and work on one strategy at a time.

## Why digital marketing is key for beauty salons in Colombia

Colombian consumer behavior has changed for good:

- **78% of people** search for a local service on Google before going to the business *(BrightLocal, Local Consumer Review Survey 2024)*.
- **94% use WhatsApp** as an everyday communication channel, including to interact with businesses *(We Are Social / DataReportal, Digital 2025: Colombia)*.
- Clients check Google reviews before choosing a new salon.
- Business discovery increasingly happens on TikTok and Instagram.

Word of mouth still matters, but today it mostly happens online: recommendations arrive on WhatsApp, photos of the result get posted to Instagram, and the friend who asks "where did you get your hair cut?" gets a link, not just a name.

## A beauty salon's marketing funnel

Before talking tactics, you need to understand the funnel: the path a client takes from not knowing you to becoming a repeat client.

```
Discovery → Consideration → First visit → Retention → Recommendation
```

Each stage needs a different strategy:

| Stage | Main channel | Key tool | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Google, TikTok, Instagram | Google Business Profile, content | Get found |
| Consideration | Reviews, photos, Google profile | Google Business Profile, reviews | Get chosen |
| First visit | WhatsApp, booking link | AgenditApp | Frictionless booking |
| Retention | Reminders, loyalty | AgenditApp | Clients come back without you chasing them |
| Recommendation | Experience + referrals | Referral program | Clients bring new clients |

Most salons focus only on the first stage (finding new clients) and neglect the rest. Winning a new client costs 5 to 7 times more than keeping an existing one. The loyalty section later on explains this in detail.

## 1. Local SEO: show up on Google when people search for you

Local SEO is the strategy of appearing in Google's top results when someone searches "beauty salon" in your city or neighborhood. It doesn't require an advertising budget: it requires time and the right setup.

### Google Business Profile: the pillar of local SEO

Your Google Business Profile is the most powerful local SEO tool. When someone searches "beauty salon in Chapinero" or "barbershop near me", Google shows a map with three results. That "local pack" captures more than 70% of the clicks.

To appear there you need:
- A verified, fully completed Google Business Profile.
- High-quality photos of the salon and your work.
- At least 20–30 reviews with an average rating above 4.0.
- Up-to-date information (hours, phone, address).
- Regular activity (replying to reviews, publishing Google posts).

[We have a step-by-step guide to setting up your Google Business Profile properly](/en/blog/google-business-profile-beauty-salon-guide) if you want to go deeper on this strategy.

### Your website: the foundation of organic SEO

Your own website complements your Google profile. For a local salon, you don't need anything complex: a page that describes your services, shows photos of your work, lists your hours and has a clearly visible online booking button.

What matters most for SEO:
- Include your city or neighborhood in the page title (e.g. "Beauty Salon in El Poblado, Medellín").
- Mention your main services by their full names.
- Add your exact address in the footer.
- Use the same name, address and phone number on your website and Google Business Profile (NAP consistency).

## 2. WhatsApp Business: communication that converts

WhatsApp isn't just a communication channel: it's the most effective sales tool there is for local businesses in Colombia. With a 98% open rate *(Infobip / Twilio, Mobile Messaging Industry Report)*, it beats every other channel by a wide margin.

For a beauty salon, WhatsApp has three main uses:

**Inquiries and customer service**: clients ask about prices, request photos of your work and check availability. Set up quick replies for the most frequent questions and a professional greeting message.

**Automatic reminders**: [automatic WhatsApp reminders reduce no-shows by up to 70%](/en/blog/reduce-no-shows-salon-whatsapp-reminders). When the system sends the reminder the day before the appointment from your business's number, confirmation rates are far higher than with any other method.

**Marketing and reactivation**: for clients who haven't been back in 60 or 90 days, a personal WhatsApp message ("Hi María, we haven't seen you in a while. Here's an exclusive discount for this week") gets far higher response rates than email.

## 3. Online booking: your biggest competitive edge

Here's one of the biggest changes a salon can make to both its operations and its marketing: **offering 24/7 online booking**.

40% of online bookings happen outside business hours: at night, on weekends, at times when the client couldn't call or wait for a WhatsApp reply. If you don't have online booking, you're losing that 40%.

From a marketing point of view, your booking link also becomes an asset you can use on every channel:
- In your Instagram and TikTok bio.
- In your WhatsApp Business profile.
- As the appointment link on your Google profile.
- On your website.
- In social media stories and posts.

Every time a potential client clicks that link and books, you turn them into a real client with no effort on your part. It's marketing that works while you're with other clients.

## 4. Social media: Instagram and TikTok for salons

Social media is the most powerful discovery channel for beauty services. Visual content (cuts, color, before/afters, nail art) gets organic reach on Instagram and TikTok that no other industry can match.

### Instagram: portfolio and community

Instagram is perfect for building a visual portfolio of your work. The most successful salon accounts in Colombia share:

- **Before/after**: the highest-engagement format in the industry. Show the full transformation.
- **Process**: videos of the work in progress: the client in the chair, the color going on, the style taking shape.
- **Behind the scenes**: the team, the salon, day-to-day life. It humanizes the business.
- **Happy clients**: with their permission, share photos of satisfied clients. People trust more when they see real people.

For your link in bio, use your booking system link. That way, whenever someone sees your content and wants to book, they can do it directly.

### TikTok: mass discovery

TikTok's discovery algorithm is very different: content can reach people who don't follow you, which makes it ideal for winning new clients. The videos that work best for salons:

- Dramatic transformations (before/after in video form).
- Hair care or beauty tips.
- "A day at the salon" vlog-style videos.
- Answers to clients' frequent questions.

You don't need professional production. A great result + good lighting + a trending sound is enough to get thousands of organic views.

## 5. Paid advertising: Google Ads and Meta Ads

Once the rest of your strategy is running, paid advertising amplifies the results. Don't use it as your first step: secure the organic fundamentals first.

### Google Ads for salons

Google Ads lets you appear at the top of search results for keywords like "beauty salon Bogotá" or "cut and color Medellín". The advantage: you reach people who are actively looking for that service right now.

A local salon's budget can start at COP 50,000–100,000 (about US$12–25) a day and be adjusted based on results.

### Meta Ads (Instagram + Facebook)

Instagram and Facebook ads are ideal for:
- Promoting discounts or new services to a targeted local audience.
- Re-engaging clients who haven't visited in a while (remarketing).
- Gaining quality followers who later become clients.

Targeting by location + beauty interests lets you reach exactly your ideal client profile.

## 6. Loyalty: the cheapest marketing there is

This is the strategy most salons underestimate, and the one with the biggest impact on revenue: keeping your existing clients.

**The data speaks for itself**:
- Increasing client retention by just 5% can increase profits by 25% to 95%.
- A repeat client spends on average 67% more than a new one.
- Acquiring a new client costs 5–7 times more than keeping one.

Loyalty at a beauty salon works on three levels:

**Level 1 — Experience**: the foundation of everything. If the in-salon experience isn't excellent, no marketing strategy can save it. Punctuality, service quality, atmosphere, personal attention.

**Level 2 — Follow-up**: appointment reminders, birthday messages, the post-service message asking whether they were happy. This level turns a single good experience into an ongoing relationship.

## 7. Email marketing: the forgotten channel

Email may seem old-fashioned compared to WhatsApp, but for some goals it's still effective, especially for more formal or detailed communication:

- A monthly newsletter with beauty trends and salon promotions.
- A reactivation campaign for inactive clients.
- Detailed appointment confirmations.

The condition is having the client's email address. If your scheduling system collects it during booking, you have a database you can work with.

## A 90-day marketing plan for a salon in Colombia

If you're just getting started, here's the recommended implementation sequence:

**Month 1 — Foundations:**
- Verify and complete your Google Business Profile.
- Set up WhatsApp Business properly (profile, quick replies, automatic messages).
- Implement an online booking system.
- Start asking happy clients for reviews.

**Month 2 — Content and community:**
- Post consistently on Instagram (at least 4 posts a week).
- Experiment with TikTok (1–2 videos a week).
- Set up automatic appointment reminders.
- Reply to every Google review.

**Month 3 — Amplification:**
- Launch your loyalty program.
- Run your first paid ad campaigns (small test budget).
- Review metrics and double down on what works.
- Set up a referral system.
