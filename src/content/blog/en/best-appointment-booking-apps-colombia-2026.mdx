The market for appointment scheduling software has grown enormously in recent years, and salon, barbershop and spa owners in Colombia have more options than ever. The problem is that many of these tools were designed for the US or European market: they come with dollar pricing, no Spanish-language support and no native WhatsApp integration.

In this article we review the **7 best appointment booking apps in Colombia for 2026**, with an honest look at their strengths, limitations and the kind of business each one suits best.

## What to look for in a booking app for your business

Before getting into the comparison, here are the criteria that actually matter for a beauty business in Colombia:

- **24/7 online booking** without you having to be there
- **Automatic WhatsApp reminders** (the channel your clients actually use)
- **Multi-staff management** if you have a team
- **Affordable pricing** in Colombian pesos or reasonable dollar amounts
- **Spanish-language support** for when something goes wrong
- **Ease of use** for you and your clients

With these criteria in mind, here's the comparison.

---

## 1. AgenditApp — Best option for Colombia and Latin America

[AgenditApp](https://agenditapp.com/en) is built specifically for the Latin American market. Unlike its global competitors, its key differentiator is **native WhatsApp integration from the business's own number**, not from a generic platform number.

**What makes it different:**
- Reminders are sent from your WhatsApp Business number, which builds more trust and gets more messages opened.
- A personalized booking page with your business's name and colors.
- Affordable dollar pricing with active Spanish-language support.
- Built for the Colombian context: barbershops, beauty salons, spas, clinics and more.

**Pros:**
- Native WhatsApp from the business's number
- Spanish-language support (Mon–Sat)
- Simple interface, up and running in under 30 minutes
- No long-term contract: cancel anytime
- Plans priced for small businesses

**Cons:**
- A younger platform than its global competitors
- Fewer third-party integrations than SimplyBook or Vagaro

**Best for:** Beauty salons, barbershops, spas, clinics and any service business in Colombia that uses WhatsApp as its main channel.

---

## 2. AgendaPro — The market leader in Latin America

AgendaPro is the best-known scheduling software in the Spanish-speaking market. It has been the region's reference for years and has a large user base in Colombia, Mexico and Chile.

**Pros:**
- Strong brand recognition and trust
- Robust features: multiple locations, advanced reports, email marketing
- Well-built mobile app for both clients and the business
- Payment gateway integrations

**Cons:**
- Higher price than newer options, especially for small businesses
- Steeper learning curve
- WhatsApp reminders depend on extra configuration or higher-tier plans
- Support can be slow during peak periods

**Best for:** Medium and large businesses with multiple professionals or locations that need advanced reporting and have the budget for it.

---

## 3. Fresha — The free option for the beauty industry

Fresha (formerly Shedul) is known worldwide for its free model. It doesn't charge a monthly subscription; it makes money through fees on payments processed on the platform.

**Pros:**
- A genuinely free plan (no limit on appointments or staff)
- Well-designed client app
- Built-in marketing tools
- Very popular in the beauty industry globally

**Cons:**
- Fees on processed payments (2.19% + fixed fee)
- Support mainly in English
- No native WhatsApp integration for the Colombian market
- The interface can be confusing for less tech-savvy users

**Best for:** Businesses that want to start without paying for software and are willing to pay fees on digital payments.

---

## 4. SimplyBook.me — The most customizable

SimplyBook.me is a Swiss platform with a broad global presence. Its biggest strength is **extreme customization**: you control almost every aspect of the booking system.

**Pros:**
- More than 50 features you can switch on or off
- Integrations with Google Calendar, Zoom and hundreds of tools
- Booking widget you can embed on any website
- Multiple languages, including Spanish

**Cons:**
- Dollar pricing (can be expensive for small businesses)
- Extreme customization can be overwhelming at first
- No direct WhatsApp integration for sending reminders
- Visually dated admin interface

**Best for:** Businesses with specific customization needs, many complex services or integrations with external tools.

---

## 5. Vagaro — Powerful but US-focused

Vagaro is one of the North American market leaders for salons, spas and gyms. It's very feature-rich but aimed mainly at English-speaking markets.

**Pros:**
- Very complete features: built-in point of sale, payroll, inventory
- Its own marketplace where clients can discover your business
- Very well-built app

**Cons:**
- Dollar pricing (USD $30+ per month)
- Support mainly in English
- No WhatsApp Business integration for the Colombian market
- The client marketplace is useful in the US, not in Colombia

**Best for:** Businesses with a US presence or that serve English-speaking clients.

---

## 6. WeiBook — A basic option to get started

WeiBook is a more basic scheduling platform available in several Latin American countries. It covers the essentials of online booking but with less depth than the options above.

**Pros:**
- Simple, easy-to-understand interface
- Affordable price
- Available in Spanish

**Cons:**
- Limited features compared with competitors
- No robust WhatsApp integration
- Slow product updates and development
- Variable support response times

**Best for:** Very small businesses (1–2 people) that only need the basics and have a minimal budget.

---

## 7. Noona — Popular in Scandinavia, limited in Colombia

Noona is a platform with Scandinavian roots that has expanded globally. It's known for its clean design and its focus on the beauty industry.

**Pros:**
- Attractive, intuitive visual design
- Good client loyalty tools
- Multi-staff management

**Cons:**
- Little Spanish-language support
- No WhatsApp integration for the Latin American market
- Pricing in euros/dollars with no local options
- Very limited Spanish-language community and resources

**Best for:** Businesses with an international clientele or in markets where English is the main language.

## Which one should you choose for your salon in Colombia?

The answer depends on your size and priorities:

**If you run a small or mid-sized salon in Colombia** and use WhatsApp as your main channel with clients, **AgenditApp is the option most aligned** with the local market. Reminders from your own number, Spanish-language support and affordable pricing make it the natural choice.

**If you need very advanced features** and have the budget, **AgendaPro** is the most mature option in the Spanish-speaking market.

**If you want to start at no cost**, **Fresha** is a reasonable alternative while your business grows, although you'll pay fees on payments.

**If you have very specific customization needs**, **SimplyBook.me** offers the most flexibility on the market.

## The criterion that matters most: will your clients actually use it?

At the end of the day, the best scheduling software is the one your clients actually use to book. If your client doesn't understand how to book online, or if the reminder comes from a number they don't recognize, the tool loses its value.

That's why, in the Colombian context, WhatsApp integration isn't a nice-to-have: it's a necessity. It's where your clients are, the channel they trust and where they actually read messages.
